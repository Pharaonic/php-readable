title: Everything you need to show values to humans
subtitle: Six focused classes with static methods. No setup, no state, no framework.
items:
  - icon: chart
    title: Readable Numbers
    text: Grouped thousands, `1.2M`-style compact numbers, percentages, ordinals and numbers spelled out in words.
  - icon: database
    title: Byte Sizes
    text: SI `KB` or binary `KiB`, never mixed, plus `Bytes::parse('10 MB')` to go back to an integer.
  - icon: tag
    title: Money
    text: ISO 4217 codes with the right minor units, so `JPY` has no decimals and `KWD` has three.
  - icon: clock
    title: Durations
    text: Seconds or two dates to `2 days 4 hours`, with long or short units and a parts limit.
  - icon: translate
    title: Optional Locales
    text: Pass a locale such as `de_DE` to use Intl. Leave it out for a deterministic English formatter.
  - icon: check-circle
    title: Predictable Edge Cases
    text: Half-up rounding, carry-over to the next unit and no `-0.00`, on every PHP version.
